# Blind judgement, phase 1: «La chiave della Torre Mozza» (Basic Fantasy RPG)

Files read, each in full (and only these four):
- giudizio-cieco/partita-1.md: Fulco, Livia, Eliandro; Ugo; no Nespola; ends at turn 18 mid-combat.
- giudizio-cieco/partita-2.md: Fulco, Livia, Eliandro; Ugo; no Nespola; ends at turn 18 mid-combat.
- giudizio-cieco/partita-3.md: Livia, Nespola, Eliandro; Nespola's private question to the goblin is answered by Zirrà's ransom; Eliandro survives; ends at turn 18.
- giudizio-cieco/partita-4.md: Livia, Nespola, Eliandro; Eliandro dies at turn 15 («Narratore: Eliandro muore.», line 247); ends at turn 18.

References: "T" = turn heading in the file, and "line" = line number in the file. Quotes are verbatim from the Italian text. Causes are guesses and are marked as such.

Method note: the four files share the same opening and the same scenes, but partita-3 and partita-4 use different narrator styles and different player paths. I checked every quote and line number against the files before writing this version. Claims that could not be found verbatim were removed.

## Partita 1

**a) Clarity at the table: 2/5**
- T2, line 27: the narrator restates the T1 question as if it were a new action: «Livia chiede a Berta quando ha visto Tonio per l'ultima volta... Livia appoggia lo scudo al muro».
- T5, line 85: «Ugo carica il mulo e se ne va verso il bosco». At T6, line 97, Ugo answers Fulco's private question as if he were still there.
- T18, line 261: «Il colpo di Zirrà, a terra, Eliandro resta immobile sul fango.» The sentence does not say whether Eliandro fell, was wounded, or died.

**b) NPCs: 3/5**
- Ugo has a clear voice and clear motives: «Un passaggio basso. Ecco cosa cercava quello stolto del prete.» (T8, line 123) and «La strada no, quella non la do.» (T10, line 153).
- Berta is consistent: «Ti ricordo, soldata. Dormivi con le pecore, senza lamenti.» (T6, line 103).
- Zirrà's tic becomes a mannerism: «ride corto» at lines 193, 227 and 251 (T13, T15, T17).

**c) Agency: 3/5**
- Good: Fulco's question about the postern (T8) gets a real answer and moves the scene.
- Weak: Eliandro's private search (T9, line 131) reaches the table as a public outcome: «Accanto ci sono quelle di un paio di stivali da ragazzo» (T9 narration, lines 135-139).
- Weak: Livia's climb (T11) fails with no alternative offered.

**d) Mystery and stakes: 4/5**
- Clues move: the priest (T5, T8), the postern (T8), bare-footed prints with long nails and boy's boot prints (T9), the goat horn coming closer (T9, T11).
- Pressure: «prima di mezzanotte» (T6, line 93) and the tower at midnight (T12).

**e) Prose for the ear: 3/5**
- Strong, clear images: «Ogni erre è rovesciata, con la pancia dalla parte sbagliata» (T2).
- Repeated blocks: «Fulco resta fermo accanto al cancello, con la mazza appoggiata al palo» (T5, line 87) and «Fulco... resta in attesa» (T9, line 141).

**f) Rhythm and ending: 2/5**
- Dead turns: T4 and T5 add almost nothing beyond the gate and the sheep.
- No ending: the file stops at T18 («Fulco, il passaggio è libero solo per un momento. Che fai?», line 267).

**g) Fun: 3/5**
- The postern and Ugo's refusals make the table want to keep going. The T2 repetition and the T18 confusion break it.

**H) Coherence: 2/5**
- Ugo leaves at T5 and answers at T6 (see a).
- T2 repeats the T1 question (see a).
- T13, line 207: Eliandro's dart is narrated as a hit (line 197: «il dardo incantato parte dritto e colpisce la goblin che avanza verso la porta») but no card for Eliandro's dart appears in that turn's card list.
- T13, line 207: Livia's «Spada lunga 23 contro CD 14, riuscita» is never narrated as a hit. The narration only says she «riceve il colpo di Grosso».
- T17, line 253: «Livia Sarti: Tiro salvezza contro la morte 20 contro CD 10, riuscita». Livia has not been hit in the file, so the death save has no cause.

**I) Fluidity: 2/5**
- Empty turns: T4 and T5 (see f).
- Repeated lines: «Zirrà ride corto» (lines 193, 227, 251); «Dal crinale scende un corno di capra» (T9, line 143; T11, line 173).

**J) Basic Fantasy fidelity: 3/5**
- Good: d20 + bonus against CD, turn-based initiative, hits and misses in cards, a death save.
- Missing: Eliandro's dart at T13 has no card.
- Absurd: the death save for Livia at T17 (see H).
- Guess: the card system follows the player's action, but not always the narrator's answer.

## Partita 2

**a) Clarity at the table: 2/5**
- The narrator quotes player actions in the third person several times: «Narratore: Fulco Granito: «Chiedo a Ugo»» (T3, line 59) and «Narratore: Fulco Granito: «Dico agli altri»» (T8, line 115). The table hears the same action twice without new information.
- T2, line 39: Berta gives a second answer to the T1 question. At T1, line 17, she had already answered: «Lui. Ha venduto ai goblin il sentiero, per vendetta.»

**b) NPCs: 3/5**
- Berta's voice is sharp: «Giuratelo sul vostro nome, non sul mio.» (T6, line 101).
- Ugo is dismissive and consistent: «Paese. Sputo, e poi lavoro. Il carbone non aspetta chi accusa.» (T11, line 159).
- The private questions from Fulco (T6, line 97) and Eliandro (T9, line 127) get no narrated answer at all.

**c) Agency: 2/5**
- Good: the bribe to Ugo (T10) works: «Occhio alle frasche, a metà salita. Sotto c'è il buco.» (line 145).
- Weak: Fulco's private question to Ugo (T6, line 97) and Eliandro's private search (T9, line 127) get no narrated response.
- Weak: Fulco's search at T8, «Cercare porte segrete e trappole 14 contro CD 13, riuscita» (line 121), produces no narrated discovery.

**d) Mystery and stakes: 3/5**
- Clues: the postern, the hole in the wood, the road behind the charcoal kiln. The mystery advances but stalls around T11.
- Pressure: Tonio on the stairs and the tower at midnight (T10).

**e) Prose for the ear: 2/5**
- Paragraphs from partita-1 return unchanged: «Ogni erre è rovesciata...», «Le lettere sono grandi e tonde...», «Nel fango ci sono impronte piccole...».
- The narrator often quotes the action instead of turning it into a scene (see a).

**f) Rhythm and ending: 2/5**
- Empty turns: T4 and T5, and T9, where the private action has no answer.
- No ending: the file stops at T18 («Scudo bianco, che braccio! Ma il mio tiene.», line 273).

**g) Fun: 2/5**
- The insults with Zirrà land well: «Sasso storto, piccolo! Tiri come una nonna, ridi tu?» (T16, line 249). The rest is flattened by absurd cards (see J).

**H) Coherence: 2/5**
- T11, line 165: «Eliandro: Tiro salvezza contro raggio della morte, con la Destrezza (Destrezza) 23 contro CD 17, riuscita». No ray appears in the narration, and nothing attacks Eliandro. The label is also duplicated: «(Destrezza) (Destrezza)».
- T14, line 203: Zirrà says «Ehi, mezza misura! Quello è scudo, mica bersaglio!». This refers to Fulco's sling shot, which the card at T13 (line 197) reports as «Fionda 5 contro CD 14, fallita». The narration has the shot strike a shield, which the card does not support.
- T13, line 197: Eliandro's dart «39 contro CD 14, riuscita» is narrated as a hit a turn later (T14, line 207: «Il dardo fende l'aria del cerchio e colpisce Zirrà.»). This is not a contradiction, only a delay.

**I) Fluidity: 2/5**
- Empty turns: T4, T5, T9 (see f).
- Narrator echoes: lines 59 and 115 (see a).

**J) Basic Fantasy fidelity: 2/5**
- Absurd cards:
  - T17, line 259: «Eliandro: Forzare una porta bloccata 9 contro CD 17, fallita». Eliandro's action (line 255) is «Anch'io gli stringo la spalla e chiedo», a squeeze on the shoulder and a question. This is the «force a door» example from the brief.
  - T11, line 165: a death-ray save with no ray (see H).
  - T18, line 277: «Livia Sarti: Cercare porte segrete e trappole 11 contro CD 17, fallita», for walking down a stair.
- Missing or unnarrated results: «Livia Sarti: Spada lunga 22 contro CD 14, riuscita» (T15, line 241) is never narrated as a hit.
- Guess: the card system maps the player's raw verb to the first skill it finds.

## Partita 3

**a) Clarity at the table: 3/5**
- Clear: speakers are named, and the scene moves in a readable way.
- Slightly confused: «Berta stringe la chiave al collo e alza il mento» (T1, line 17) differs from the opening's «con una chiave di ferro stretta nel pugno».

**b) NPCs: 4/5**
- Zirrà has the sharpest voice: «Ehi! Chi vi ha detto di entrare? Mezzanotte è tra poco, e voi fate casino.» (T13, line 245) and «Il ragazzo è mio. L'ho preso nel fienile, mentre dormiva.» (T12, line 217).
- Zirrà answers Eliandro's public question in character: «Lettere? Le abbiamo rubate al vecchio prete, scudo bianco.» (T16, line 297).
- Berta and Ugo are consistent, though Berta's voice is shaken by private questions (see Violations below).

**c) Agency: 2/5**
- Good: Eliandro's public question about the letters (T16) gets a clear answer.
- Weak: Livia's private question to Berta (T5) is answered aloud (see H and violations).

**d) Mystery and stakes: 4/5**
- Tonio is alive and on the stairs, and Zirrà's ransom («Costa di più, adesso. Tutta la cassa», line 307) raises the stakes.
- The midnight deadline (T13, line 245) keeps pressure on.

**e) Prose for the ear: 3/5**
- Short, clear sentences in most places.
- Repeated narrator lines: «Zirrà incassa il colpo» and «Eliandro incassa il colpo» are sometimes filler.

**f) Rhythm and ending: 3/5**
- The ransom hook is a good turn to end on, but the file stops at T18 with a dice card and no outcome («Scassinare serrature 10 contro CD 17, fallita», line 329).

**g) Fun: 3/5**
- Zirrà's ransom and Tonio's presence make the table want more. The leaked private lines reduce trust.

**H) Coherence: 2/5**
- T5, lines 87 and 91: Berta answers Livia's private question aloud, after Livia's private line (line 81: «Mi avvicino a Berta: «Il balivo ti prende l'ovile alla prima neve, lo sai?»»). Berta's reply repeats the private content: «Il balivo sta a Valdirame» and «Se perdo l'ovile, perdo anche lui. Lo capisci o no?»
- T6, line 107: Berta answers Eliandro's private question aloud: «Qui nessuno parla coi goblin. Pensa che io abbia tempo per le tue domande?» (private line at line 103).
- T13, line 237: Eliandro's dart is narrated as a hit ("Il dardo incantato parte dritto verso il goblin più vicino, e lo colpisce al fianco"), but the card list at line 253 has no Eliandro dart.

**I) Fluidity: 3/5**
- Narrator repetitions around T13-T14 (the fight is written in similar lines).
- The mid-session «Sullo schermo» screens are fine here.

**J) Basic Fantasy fidelity: 3/5**
- Good: attack and skill cards use CD and show success or failure. The death save, which appears in partita-4, is not needed here.
- Absurd: none on the scale of partita-2. The card «Forzare una porta bloccata 26 contro CD 17, riuscita» (line 253) for forcing a door is sensible.

**Violations of privacy (the most serious in this file):**
- T5: Livia's private line is answered aloud by Berta (lines 87, 91; see H).
- T6: Eliandro's private question is answered aloud by Berta (line 107; see H).
- T17, line 307: Nespola's private question («Il ragazzo, Tonio. Dov'è?», line 303) is answered by Zirrà aloud, and Zirrà repeats the question's content: «Vuoi sapere dov'è il ragazzo, piccola?»

## Partita 4

**a) Clarity at the table: 3/5**
- The narrator often reports actions in quotes with the player's name: «Narratore: Livia Sarti: «Vedo i goblin davanti alla porta della torre»».
- The scene screen «Sullo schermo: La scena è finita: il master sceglie come andare avanti» (T5, line 73) appears mid-session without a narrative bridge.

**b) NPCs: 4/5**
- Zirrà: «Furba, mezza misura!» (T13, line 201) and «Alle spalle, mezza misura? Furba!» (T14, line 229). Good voice, but the phrase repeats.
- The boy (Tonio) gives the most important clues in the file: «Dice che la decima non è pagata. Alla prima neve, dice, il balivo si prende l'ovile.» (T16, line 259).
- Grosso: «Fame. Sempre fame.» (T11, line 167), consistent.

**c) Agency: 3/5**
- Good: Livia's direct question to the boy (T16) gets a clear answer, and the boy's confession moves the plot.
- Weak: Nespola's private question to the goblin (T17) gets only «Nespola incassa il colpo.» (line 269), a non-answer.

**d) Mystery and stakes: 4/5**
- The mystery resolves into a motive: the unpaid tithe, the silver in the crypt, and Tonio's reasons. The door and the chest are concrete stakes.
- Eliandro's death at T15 (line 247) is a real cost.

**e) Prose for the ear: 3/5**
- Short and clear sentences. The quote-style narration of actions repeats the problem seen in partita-2.

**f) Rhythm and ending: 3/5**
- Eliandro's death and the boy's confession give the file a real turn.
- It ends at T18 with a card (line 283) and a dropped stone, not an ending.

**g) Fun: 3/5**
- The tension around the boy and the death of Eliandro carry the file. The absurd card at T18 and the unanswered private question at T17 undercut it.

**H) Coherence: 2/5**
- T6, lines 83 and 87: the card says «Livia Sarti: Carisma 13 contro CD 17, fallita», but the narration says «Berta si sfila il laccio dal collo e vi mette in mano la chiave.» A failed check gives the key.
- T17, line 269: a private question gets an unrelated public line («Nespola incassa il colpo.»).
- T15, lines 247-249: «Eliandro muore.» with the death save «Tiro salvezza contro la morte 8 contro CD 10, fallita». Consistent with the rule, but the earlier hits on Eliandro (T13 line 211, T14 line 233) are not tracked in the file.

**I) Fluidity: 3/5**
- The tic «mezza misura» (lines 201, 229) and «Io aspetto, la luna no» (line 193) are repeated.
- The mid-session scene screen (line 73) interrupts the flow.

**J) Basic Fantasy fidelity: 3/5**
- Good: attacks against CD, skill checks, and a death save at zero hit points.
- Absurd: T18, line 283, «Livia Sarti: Scalare pareti 5 contro CD 17, fallita» for walking down a cellar staircase.
- Guess: the card system chooses a skill from the action's verb, as in partita-2.

## Top 8 problems a player notices (most damaging first)

1. **Private actions answered aloud or echoed (P3 T5, T6, T17; P1 T6, T9).** Berta answers Livia's private question at P3 T5 (lines 87, 91) and Eliandro's at P3 T6 (line 107). Zirrà repeats Nespola's private question at P3 T17 (line 307). Ugo gives Fulco the route aloud at P1 T6 (line 97). Eliandro's private search outcome is narrated to everyone at P1 T9 (lines 135-139).
2. **Absurd or phantom dice cards (P2 T11 line 165, T17 line 259, T18 line 277; P1 T17 line 253; P4 T18 line 283).** A door force for a shoulder squeeze, a death-ray save with no ray, a death save for a character who was not hit, a «Scalare pareti» check to go down stairs.
3. **Hits narrated without a card, or cards without a narrated result (P1 T13, lines 197 and 207; P3 T13, lines 237 and 253; P2 T15, line 241).** The dart and sword results are missing from the table's view.
4. **Stale and echoed lines (P1 T2 line 27; P2 T2 line 39; P2 T3 line 59 and T8 line 115).** The narrator restates the previous question or quotes the player's action without moving the scene.
5. **Characters who speak after leaving the scene (P1 T5 line 85, then T6 line 97).** Ugo carries the mule away and then answers Fulco as if he were present.
6. **Card and narration disagree (P4 T6, lines 83 and 87).** A failed Carisma check is followed by Berta handing over the key.
7. **Verbal tics that replace content (Zirrà: «ride corto» P1 lines 193, 227, 251; «mezza misura» P2 line 203, P4 lines 201, 229; «la luna no» P2 line 225, P4 line 193).**
8. **No partita reaches an ending (all four stop at T18 mid-scene).** None of the four files has a closing beat; they end on a card or a question.

## Guesses about causes (not verified in code)

- The card system maps the player's raw verb to the first matching skill (partita-2 T11, T17, T18; partita-4 T18).
- The narrator receives the action log and restates it, which produces echoes and stale lines (partita-1 T2; partita-2 T3, T8).
- Private answers may be routed by the narrator as ordinary dialogue, which breaks the privacy rule (partita-3 T5, T6, T17).
