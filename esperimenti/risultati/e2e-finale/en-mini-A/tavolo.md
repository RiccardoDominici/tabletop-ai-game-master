# Al tavolo

Ciò che i giocatori hanno sentito e visto, nell’ordine: le righe dette ad alta voce dal narratore e dai personaggi, le azioni dei giocatori come compaiono sullo schermo, gli avvisi dello schermo. Le azioni private le conoscono solo chi le ha scritte e il narratore: sono marcate «azione privata».

## Apertura

**Narrator:** It has rained for three days on the valley. The inn is full of smoke. Mr. Hale, the innkeeper, looks at you: what do you do?

## Turno 1

**Mara Quill** (giocatore): I walk up to the counter and ask Mr. Hale for two rooms for the night.

**Tobin Ash** (giocatore): I sit by the fire and listen to what the guard says.

**Narrator:** Mara walks up to the counter and asks for two rooms for the night.

**Hale the Innkeeper:** Two rooms. The upper floor is damp, but the beds are dry.

**Narrator:** Hale turns a key on its hook without looking at it.

**Narrator:** Tobin sits by the fire. Lia, the guard, leans in at the next table and speaks low.

**Guard Lia:** Road's closed past the ford. Nobody crosses before the water drops.

**Narrator:** Lia's eyes slide toward the counter, then back to her cup.

## Turno 2

**Tobin Ash** (giocatore): I tell the guard we are only passing through and mean no trouble.

**Mara Quill** (giocatore): Me too, I nod at the guard and keep my hands away from my sword.

**Narrator:** Tobin tells the guard they are only passing through, and means no trouble.

**Guard Lia:** Passing through. Everybody's passing through this week. Lia's gaze hangs on Tobin's hands, then drops.

**Narrator:** Mara nods at the guard and keeps her hands well away from her sword.

**Narrator:** Lia takes that nod for what it is, and her cup stops halfway to her mouth.

**Narrator:** Far to the north, thunder rolls once, low and distant, and every head in the room turns toward the shutters.

## Turno 3

**Mara Quill** (giocatore): I lean over the counter and look behind it.

**Tobin Ash** (giocatore, *azione privata*): I ask the innkeeper quietly what he is hiding.

**Narrator:** Mara leans over the counter and looks behind it.

**Narrator:** Behind the counter, beside the wine casks, a clay jug sits half-hidden under a cloth.

**Narrator:** Mara, the jug is still wet at the rim, and it smells of river water.

**Narrator:** Tobin asks the innkeeper, quietly, what he is hiding.

**Hale the Innkeeper:** Hiding? I hide nothing but the rain. Ask me again and I'll charge you for the question.

**Narrator:** Hale's rag stops on the counter, and his thumb rubs the wood.
