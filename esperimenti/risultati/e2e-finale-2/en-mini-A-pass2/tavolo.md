# Al tavolo

Ciò che i giocatori hanno sentito e visto, nell’ordine: le righe dette ad alta voce dal narratore e dai personaggi, le azioni dei giocatori come compaiono sullo schermo, gli avvisi dello schermo. Le azioni private le conoscono solo chi le ha scritte e il narratore: sono marcate «azione privata».

## Apertura

**Narrator:** It has rained for three days on the valley. The inn is full of smoke. Mr. Hale, the innkeeper, looks at you: what do you do?

## Turno 1

**Mara Quill** (giocatore): I walk up to the counter and ask Mr. Hale for two rooms for the night.

**Tobin Ash** (giocatore): I sit by the fire and listen to what the guard says.

**Narrator:** Mara Quill walks up to the counter and asks Mr. Hale for two rooms for the night.

**Hale the Innkeeper:** Two rooms. Cash first, the roof is leaking anyway.

**Narrator:** Hale slides a ledger across the wood and waits, thumb resting on the page.

**Narrator:** Tobin Ash sits by the fire. Guard Lia mutters to her cup, and the log cracks.

**Guard Lia:** Three days of rain. The river's already over the low path.

**Narrator:** Behind the counter, a clay jug sits half-hidden under a cloth.

## Turno 2

**Tobin Ash** (giocatore): I tell the guard we are only passing through and mean no trouble.

**Mara Quill** (giocatore): Me too, I nod at the guard and keep my hands away from my sword.

**Narrator:** Tobin Ash tells the guard you are only passing through and mean no trouble.

**Guard Lia:** Passing through. Everyone is passing through when the river rises.

**Narrator:** Mara Quill nods at the guard and keeps her hands away from her sword.

**Narrator:** Guard Lia studies the nod, then slowly turns her cup a quarter turn on the wood.

**Narrator:** Distant thunder rolls over the hills, and the shutters rattle once against the wall.

**Narrator:** Hale stops with the ledger half-open, pen still in his hand. What do you do?

## Turno 3

**Mara Quill** (giocatore): I lean over the counter and look behind it.

**Tobin Ash** (giocatore, *azione privata*): I ask the innkeeper quietly what he is hiding.

**Narrator:** Mara Quill leans over the counter and looks behind it.

**Narrator:** Behind the counter, under the cloth, the clay jug is there. Its wax seal is broken.

**Hale the Innkeeper:** Mind your elbows, lady. That's just my cellar wine.

**Narrator:** Hale's thumb tightens on the ledger page.

**Narrator:** Tobin Ash asks the innkeeper quietly what he is hiding.

**Hale the Innkeeper:** Nothing you'd want to pay for, friend.

**Narrator:** Guard Lia's cup stops turning.
