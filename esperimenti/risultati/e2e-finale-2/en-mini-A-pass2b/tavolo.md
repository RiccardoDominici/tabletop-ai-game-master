# Al tavolo

Ciò che i giocatori hanno sentito e visto, nell’ordine: le righe dette ad alta voce dal narratore e dai personaggi, le azioni dei giocatori come compaiono sullo schermo, gli avvisi dello schermo. Le azioni private le conoscono solo chi le ha scritte e il narratore: sono marcate «azione privata».

## Apertura

**Narrator:** It has rained for three days on the valley. The inn is full of smoke. Mr. Hale, the innkeeper, looks at you: what do you do?

## Turno 1

**Mara Quill** (giocatore): I walk up to the counter and ask Mr. Hale for two rooms for the night.

**Tobin Ash** (giocatore): I sit by the fire and listen to what the guard says.

**Narrator:** Mara walks up to the counter and asks Mr. Hale for two rooms for the night.

**Hale the Innkeeper:** Two rooms. Coins first, then keys.

**Narrator:** Hale slides a dented jug behind the counter, out of sight, and pulls two keys from a hook.

**Narrator:** Tobin sits by the fire. Guard Lia is talking low, and the wood cracks loudly.

**Guard Lia:** The road north is shut. Nobody goes up past the mill until the water drops.

**Narrator:** Mara, the keys are on the counter, but Hale's hand stays on them. What do you do?

## Turno 2

**Tobin Ash** (giocatore): I tell the guard we are only passing through and mean no trouble.

**Mara Quill** (giocatore): Me too, I nod at the guard and keep my hands away from my sword.

**Narrator:** Tobin tells the guard you are only passing through and mean no trouble.

**Guard Lia:** Everyone passing through says that. Keep your hands where I can see them.

**Narrator:** Mara nods at the guard and keeps her hands away from her sword.

**Narrator:** Distant thunder rolls over the hills, and the door slams in the wind.

**Narrator:** Guard Lia's eyes stay on Mara's hands a moment longer than they need to. Hale's fingers still rest on the keys. What do you do?

## Turno 3

**Mara Quill** (giocatore): I lean over the counter and look behind it.

**Tobin Ash** (giocatore, *azione privata*): I ask the innkeeper quietly what he is hiding.

**Narrator:** Mara leans over the counter and looks behind it.

**Narrator:** Behind the counter, beside a stack of casks, a dented jug sits on its side, a damp ring under it.

**Narrator:** Hale's eyes flick toward the cellar, then back to his hands on the keys.

**Narrator:** Guard Lia pushes her chair back a little, listening.
