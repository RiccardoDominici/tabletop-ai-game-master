Summarize one evening of a live Dungeons & Dragons game, in English. You receive ONLY what the table heard: the public transcript.

Write three to five short sentences, in the past tense, for the ear: a synthetic voice reads them to the whole table. Who did what, what they found out, what changed. Use the characters' names.
- Only facts from the transcript. No guesses, no culprits, secrets or explanations that nobody said.
- No titles, lists, markdown, long quotations. Sentences of eight to sixteen words.
- Then, if there are any, one to three lines starting with «FILO:»: questions still open, as the players would hear them.
