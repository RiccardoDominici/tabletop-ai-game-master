# Character creation at the table (rules-engine package R5, 22 November 2026)

Riccardo (20 November): «la fase di creazione personaggio? i tiri iniziali?» (the character creation phase? the starting
rolls?). Until this package the PCs were only pregenerated by the Preparer, and nothing checked them against the
manual's races and classes (audit gap 7, mechanism M7, docs/regole-audit/basic-fantasy-personaggio.md).

Now:
- the manual's creation procedure is **rule-set data** (regole.yaml → `creazione`, docs/regole.md §2 «Creazione»),
  general for any manual: the ordered steps, how scores are generated, the Game Master's options, the first level's hit
  points, the starting money, a caster's starting book, the languages; races and classes carry their requirements and
  allow-lists;
- **one check** judges every PC's sheet against it (regole/controllo-pg.ts; the validator's mirror
  strumenti/valida/regole_creazione.py): a pregenerated PC the Preparer writes and a PC built at the table pass the
  same rules;
- each player can **build a PC on the phone**, step by step, with real dice; the Game Master's panel follows every draft,
  approves or undoes a step and approves the finished PC, which joins the campaign at once and in every session after.

## 1. The campaign's choice

`campagna.yaml → regole.creazione` (creazione/opzioni.ts; validator `SCHEDA.regole` and `REGOLE.creazione`):

```yaml
regole:
  tiri_giocatori: manuale       # the dice of creation follow it too: real dice entered on the phone (default)
  creazione:
    modo: entrambi              # pregenerati (default: as before) | al_tavolo (pg.yaml empty, every PC from a phone) | entrambi
    metodo: in_ordine           # one of the set's creazione.caratteristiche.metodi (default: the first)
    opzioni: [scambio_punti]    # the set's options the Game Master allows (default: none; Basic Fantasy p. 186)
    senza_speranza: [ritira]    # the hopeless character's remedies allowed (default: the set's)
    punti_ferita: massimo       # the first level's die: tira | massimo (default: the set's)
    livello: 3                  # the level new PCs start at (default: the set's; a player who joins a grown party)
    incantesimi: master         # who picks a caster's starting spells: scelta | master | tiro (default: the set's)
```

Creation at the table needs a set with `creazione` (the validator stops `al_tavolo` or `entrambi` without one; the
server falls back to pregenerated PCs and logs it). With `al_tavolo` pg.yaml may have no PC: the campaign loads, and
«Inizia» on the projector is refused until the first PC is approved («Nessun personaggio ancora…»).

## 2. The draft (apps/server/src/creazione/)

One draft per phone, keyed by the phone's device token (the one that already remembers its PC). The modules:

| file | what it does |
|---|---|
| `bozza.ts` | the draft's data (plain JSON) and the rules that read it: the scores with the race's adjustments, the races and classes the scores open (with the reason when closed), the hopeless character, hit points by level, the money, the sheet the draft makes |
| `tiri.ts` | the next roll the current step waits for: each score's dice in order («Forza»: 3d6), each level's hit die («Punti ferita del 1° livello»: 1d8 +1), the starting money |
| `azioni.ts` | every action of the phone, checked: refused with a reason the phone shows, the draft changes only when legal |
| `acquisti.ts` | the purchases, replayed on the rolled coins with the Engine's own inventory (prove/inventario.ts): the same prices, packs, change, weights, load bands, movement, AC and attack lines as in play |
| `vista.ts` | what the phone and the panel see (`VistaCreazione`, packages/shared/src/creazione.ts), with the creation check's problems once the draft is finished |
| `voce.ts` | the approved draft as a pg.yaml entry, with `creazione` (the method, every roll and who entered it) |
| `servizio.ts` | the drafts of every phone, their actions, the Game Master's commands, the approval |
| `archivio.ts` | the store: `pg-creati/<id>.yaml` and the drafts in `pg-creati/bozze/` |
| `opzioni.ts`, `collega.ts` | the campaign's choice; building the service for a server |

**The steps** run in the set's order (`creazione.passi`, a closed list: `caratteristiche`, `razza`, `classe`,
`incantesimi`, `punti_ferita`, `monete`, `acquisti`, `lingue`, `descrizione`); an action on another step than the current
one is refused. A step closes:
- `caratteristiche` when the player confirms (after the rolls, the placing, the Game Master's options); refused while no
  race and class fit the scores (the hopeless character: the campaign's remedies, roll again or flip every score to
  `ribalta − score`, p. 187);
- `razza`, `classe` at a legal choice; the illegal ones are listed greyed with their reason and refused by the server
  («Non puoi essere Piccolo: Forza 16, serve ≤ 15.», «Un Piccolo non può essere Arcano.»);
- `incantesimi` when the book and today's spells are legal: the set's fixed spells (read magic) and exactly `a_scelta`
  more of the class's list and of the allowed spell level, today's from the book and no more than the starting level's
  slots; it closes by itself for a class with no spells at that level (a Basic Fantasy cleric); with `come: tiro` the
  Engine draws the book's spells, with `come: master` the panel gives them;
- `punti_ferita` and `monete` at their last roll; with `punti_ferita: massimo` the first level's die counts its maximum
  and nothing is rolled;
- `acquisti` when the player confirms; an item the race or class may not use at all (`vietato`) is refused, one with a
  penalty is bought with the penalty shown («Arcano: −4 a colpire»); giving one back returns exactly what it cost;
- `lingue` at the race's own languages and up to the bonus more (open slots allowed), `descrizione` at a name.

**Dice.** With manual rolls (the campaign's `regole.tiri_giocatori`, default) the player rolls real dice and enters each
one; the server refuses the wrong number of dice or a die off its faces. With automatic rolls the Engine rolls them with
the draft's **own** dice (a seed saved with the draft; from `TAGM_SEME_DADI` in a test), never the session's: a seeded
replay of a session is not shifted by a phone building a PC. Every roll is kept (`tiri`), for the panel and the PC's file.

**Undoing.** A player can redo a step (and the steps that read it: the race resets the class, the class its spells and
hit points), but not one the Game Master approved, and not a step whose dice depend on it once rolled: changing the class
after the hit points would roll them again. The Game Master's «annulla» undoes any step and throws its dice away (a
reroll is the Game Master's call); the money does not read the class, so it stays rolled.

**The check.** A finished draft is read like any PC's sheet (regole/schede.ts: AC and attack lines from the gear, attack
bonus, saves and thief values from the class tables) and checked by `controllaPersonaggio` in its strict form (built at
the table: the procedure's level and that level's XP, exactly the book's starting spells). The panel's «Approva il
personaggio» is refused while a grave problem remains.

## 3. The approved PC joins

The approval writes the PC as one pg.yaml entry in the store and the server **rebuilds itself on the same session
package** (ciclo-server.ts `ricarica`, the path a restart already takes: the session in progress resumes from its
snapshot). So the PC is everywhere at once — the phones' list, the Engine's sheets, the narrator's context, the log's
`pg_alla_fine` — and the sessions after load it like a pregenerated one (campagna/caricatore.ts merges pg.yaml and the
store). The phone that built it picks it by itself. The rebuild waits for a quiet moment (creazione/collega.ts
`perchéAspettare`): the approval is accepted before the start and in an open window where nobody has sent an action yet;
it is refused while a turn is opened, narrated or rolled, once an action was sent in the window, and while the Game
Master holds the story (pause, stop).

**Mid-session** the session resumes from its snapshot, as after any restart: the table's state is kept (the test changes
a PC's hit points from the panel and finds them after the rebuild), and the projector's page sends «inizia» again by
itself when it reconnects. The window open at the approval comes back as it was: when the narrator had invited
everyone, the new PC enters it as soon as its phone takes it; when the narrator had named PCs, the new PC's phone can
write at once, like any PC outside the invitation, and the next window calls it (azioni/tavolo.ts `aggiungiInvitato`).

**The validator** reads the PCs built at the table with the campaign's and holds their sheets to the set (strictly, as
built at the table), but not to the checks of prepared content — an arc, every NPC's attitude towards it, its name's
pronunciation, secret words in its public fields (strumenti/valida/carica.py `pg_preparati`): the campaign was written
before the PC existed, and its words are the player's. The merged view of a session package (strumenti/unisci_pacchetto.ts)
links the campaign's `regole/` too, so a package is checked against the manual's set, not the SRD.

**Where the PCs live** (creazione/archivio.ts `cartellaPgCreati`): `pg-creati/` of the play folder — the campaign's
folder for the real game (`campagne/<id>/pg-creati/`), the instance's log folder for a test instance
(`logs/porta-<porta>/pg-creati/`, the rule of `Campagna.cartellaGioco`), or `TAGM_PG_CREATI`. **`campagne/*/pg-creati/`
is in .gitignore**: a PC built at the table is play data and may carry a player's real name, so it never enters the
public repository. The validator (strumenti/valida/carica.py) and strumenti/tra_sessioni.py read the same folder
(`TAGM_PG_CREATI`, else the campaign's `pg-creati/`); the session log (registro.yaml) gets them from the server.

**A player who joins later** builds a PC the same way, at the starting level (`regole.creazione.livello`, else the set's):
at a higher level each level's hit die is rolled in turn and the XP are that level's threshold.

## 4. Transport (packages/shared/src/creazione.ts)

- `creazione:azione` (phone → server, with ack): `{dispositivo, azione}`; the ack carries the whole draft or the refusal.
- `creazione:bozza` (server → the phone's room `creazione:<dispositivo>`): the draft after a change the phone did not ask
  for (the Game Master's approval or undo).
- master command `{tipo: 'creazione', azione: approva | approva_passo | annulla_passo | scarta | incantesimi, id, passo?,
  libro?}`; `StatoMaster.creazione`: every draft with the phone's first characters and the rolls entered.
- `StatoTavolo.creazione: {modo}` when the campaign allows creation at the table.

## 5. Screens

- **Phone** (apps/web/src/telefono/creazione/): the character choice shows **Crea un personaggio** when the table state
  has `creazione`; the creation screen covers it with the set's steps in order (done, approved by the Game Master, the
  current one, «rifai» on the choice steps), the current step's screen — the dice card (one box per die with manual rolls,
  one «Tira» button with automatic ones), the races and classes with the closed ones greyed and their reason, the book
  and today's spells, the purchase screen (the catalog by kind with price and weight, «+» and «−», the coins left, the
  weight with the load band and the movement, the Armor Class and the attack lines), the languages, the name — and the
  Engine's refusal when there is one. Leaving with «Indietro» keeps the draft; reopening finds it. Approved, the phone
  remembers the new PC and takes it when the server is back (the server also binds the phone's device to it).
- **Panel** (apps/web/src/master/SezioneCreazione.tsx, «Personaggi in creazione»): one card per draft with the steps and
  their summaries, «Approva» and «Annulla» per step, the dice typed on the phone, the check's problems, «Approva il
  personaggio», «Scarta», and the book's spells when the set says the Game Master gives them.

- apps/server/test/creazione-controllo.test.ts and strumenti/test_valida_creazione.py: the shared case file
  apps/server/test/fixtures/creazione/casi.yaml (the same sheets, the same problem codes in both); the validator on the

- apps/server/test/creazione-bozza.test.ts: the draft's rules on the invented fucina fixture.
- apps/server/test/creazione-server.test.ts: the whole server — a phone builds a PC with manual dice over the socket, an
  illegal choice refused, the panel approves, the store in the test instance's logs (not the campaign), the rebuild puts
  the PC at the table and the phone's device is bound to it, a new start loads it; a PC approved mid-session (the session
  resumes, a changed hit point total stays, the new PC writes at once and is called from the next window); when an
  approval may happen; a campaign `al_tavolo` loads with no PC and does not start.
- apps/web/e2e/creazione.spec.ts, the live check on real pages (esperimenti/risultati/regole-r5/, `rifai.sh`): two phones
  build a fighter and a caster with manual dice on a two-session copy of the fucina fixture, one illegal race refused,
  the purchases change the Armor Class and the load, the Game Master approves a step and both PCs, session 1 is played
  and closed, the between-session step writes session 2 from the log, and session 2 starts with both PCs; then an elf
  magic-user built by Basic Fantasy's rules on a copy of torre-mozza.
