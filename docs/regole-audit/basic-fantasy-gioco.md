# Basic Fantasy r142 against the game: the play half (Parts 4 to 8)

Rules taken from Chris Gonnerman's *Basic Fantasy Role-Playing Game*, 4th edition, release 142 (CC BY-SA 4.0, basicfantasy.org).
This audit summarises and quotes the rules briefly, and it is shared under the same licence.

This file is a rule-by-rule map of Parts 4 to 8 of the manual (adventure, encounter and combat, monsters, treasure,
GM information) against what the Engine and the data really do today (HEAD of 20 November 2026). The companion
file `docs/regole-audit/basic-fantasy-personaggio.md`, written in parallel, covers character creation, classes,
equipment and spells (Parts 1 to 3).
Some rows overlap with it: the attack bonus and saving throw tables, Turn Undead, and the Part 8 options about
characters and spells. Those rows are marked **[overlap]**. The ids are `P<part>-<n>`.

**Method.** I read the manual text with `pdftotext`, page by page, and re-read every table in layout mode. Page
numbers are the printed ones (PDF page = printed page + 4). Then I read the Engine (`apps/server/src/regole/`,
`prove/`), the schema (docs/regole.md §2), the torre-mozza rule set built from this manual
(`campagne/torre-mozza/regole/regole.yaml`, `estratti/`, `pg.yaml`, `png/`), and the tests (`apps/server/test/`,
`strumenti/test_valida_regole.py`). "Live run" means a recorded run of the real server on torre-mozza
(docs/prove-verifica.md §6b, 15 November: attack, damage, initiative, death save, morale, flight and surprise seen at
the table). In the "where" column, a set key comes first (the general schema), then the torre-mozza value as the
instance. "Engine" means the deterministic code; "arbiter" means the model that picks which check to roll.

**Statuses** (the column `status`, five values, the same codes as the companion file; the brief's names in brackets):

| code | meaning |
|---|---|
| `impl+test` (implemented and tested) | the Engine computes the rule, and a unit test or a live run proves it |
| `impl` (implemented, untested) | the Engine computes the rule; no test proves this case |
| `sheet` (sheet data only) | the number is right only because the Preparer wrote it by hand on a sheet; the Engine just adds it |
| `narrated` (narrated only) | the rule exists only as text (set `sottosistemi`, `condizioni[].effetto`, `estratti/`, `combattimento.azioni`) for the narrator and the arbiter; no number changes |
| `absent` (absent) | the rule is not in the set or the code |

When the mechanism exists but its numbers differ from the manual, the status stays and the gap column starts with
**DEVIATES** (manual X, game Y).

### The 10 most important gaps for play at the table

Ranked by how often the rule comes up at a table, and by whether the game silently gives a wrong number.

1. **Damage and healing outside attacks never reach the HP** (P4-13b, P5-34, P5-50, P5-53, P7-19, P8-30). Traps,
   falls, burning oil, potions, healing spells and rest change nothing. Only an attack between two sheets
   (prove/risolutore-regole.ts:319, prove/risolutore.ts:462) and the GM panel (prove/motore.ts:932-939) do. General
   mechanism: one Engine effect, a damage or healing formula with an optional save, applied to a sheet. Scene checks
   (on failure), items, arbiter-classified actions and a rest command would all call it.
2. **A monster attacks once per round** (P6-07). prove/motore.ts:795-797 takes the first weapon that fits, so a
   ghoul's «2 claws, 1 bite» becomes one attack. Its damage is silently cut by half or more. General mechanism: attack
   routines on the sheet, all resolved in the NPC's step.
3. **Monster special attacks do nothing** (P6-16 to P6-21, P6-27, P5-49): poison save-or-die, ghoul paralysis,
   petrification, energy drain, breath weapons with a save for half, charm and fear. General mechanism: attack riders
   (a save plus an effect or a condition), area attacks with a save for half, uses per day, and conditions measured
   in game turns that outlive the fight. Today conditions exist only during a fight (prove/motore.ts:942-946).
4. **Saves lack their ability adjustments** (P5-61b). Poison should add Con, charm Wis and illusions Int; the set's
   `ts_morte` and `ts_incantesimi` add nothing. These are common saves, silently wrong by up to ±3. The fix is data
   only: split the abilities in the set, and the schema already supports it.
5. **Death and dying** (P8-14, P8-15). Every PC rolls a fixed 10 instead of its own Death Ray save (7 to 13 among
   torre-mozza's PCs). A success stabilises at once: there is no 2d10-round window and no binding of wounds. Healing
   from 0 has no week-long first point. General mechanism: `tiri_morte.abilita` and `tiri_morte.finestra`.
6. **No non-lethal fighting** (P5-41, P5-42, P5-43). Players often want prisoners, but there is no subduing damage.
   Fists do 1d4 killing damage, and a grab is a GM-only condition with the wrong numbers. General mechanism: an
   attack mode `non_letale` with a second damage pool, and conditions set by the arbiter after a hit.
7. **Turn Undead is text only** (P5-47, P5-48): no table, no 2d6 HD affected, no destruction. It is the cleric's
   signature move at the table. General mechanism: a set table and an Engine routine. **[overlap]**
8. **No XP, treasure or advancement** (P4-38 to P4-42, P6-14, P7-01 to P7-14, P5-19, P5-60). A campaign cannot grow:
   the attack bonus, saves and HP stay at the level the Preparer wrote. General mechanism: an XP ledger fed by the
   sheets defeated and by treasure, a value and a purse on objects and sheets, and progression tables by class and
   level with a level-up step between sessions.
9. **Situational numbers are fixed at ±2** (P5-28, P5-29, P5-43a, P5-54a, P5-54b). The manual gives −4/−4 for
   blindness, −1 to −8 for cover, −4 AC against others when held, −5 for shooting into melee and −4 against an
   invisible foe. General mechanism: condition effects and modifiers that carry a value.
10. **Morale timing** (P5-45, P5-46, P6-29). The first check comes at the first enemy down rather than at the first
    opposition. A lone monster is never checked at half its HP, the group rolls against its lowest morale, and nobody
    surrenders. General mechanism: `fine.morale.quando` and a half-HP trigger.

Next in line: per-creature surprise and ambushes (P5-03, P5-04, P6-28: done by R4); the ability-roll target that should drop with
level (P8-18); the crossbow's rate of fire (P5-30); listening for non-thieves (P4-12); the parting shot when fleeing
(P5-14); light and the game clock (P4-01, P4-17).

### Answers to the six questions

1. **Is there a character creation phase?** No. PCs are pregenerated by the Preparer (.claude/skills/preparatore/SKILL.md:79,
   «pregenerated PCs»), with fixed scores, HP and AC in `pg.yaml`. torre-mozza's pg.yaml:8 says «Punti ferita: dado
   vita al massimo più la Costituzione (decisione nel brief)». No code rolls abilities, checks race or class
   requirements, rolls HP or starting money, or buys equipment: a grep for a creation flow over apps/server/src,
   apps/web/src and packages/shared/src finds only a comment (regole/tipi.ts:5). Details are in the companion file.
2. **Are hit points tracked and changed everywhere?** They are tracked: one value per sheet in the Engine state
   (prove/stato.ts:43-58), never below 0 (:109-115), healed up to the maximum (:118-125), and saved in the session
   snapshot (:141-147). They change only through attacks between sheets (prove/risolutore-regole.ts:319 for set
   rules, prove/risolutore.ts:462 for the SRD), the GM panel's `pf` command (prove/motore.ts:932-939) and the SRD's
   natural-20 death save (prove/combattimento.ts:531, off in Basic Fantasy). Nothing else touches them: falls, traps,
   poison, oil, potions, spells, rest, starvation (gap 1). Max HP is the sheet's `pf`, never rolled.
3. **Are spell slots or spells per day tracked and consumed?** No. torre-mozza regole.yaml:75 says «Una volta al
   giorno: il Motore non lo conta, lo dice la scheda». The header comment lists «il numero di incantesimi al giorno»
   as left out, and `sottosistemi.incantesimi` is text only. The magic missile is an `automatico` weapon that can be
   used without limit. Details are in the companion file.
4. **Are weapons and armour read from data?** Partly. Damage comes from the sheet's `attacchi[].danno`
   (prove/risolutore-regole.ts:313-320). `distanza: true` picks the missile phase (prove/motore.ts:795), and the
   target's `difesa` is the AC to beat. All of it is written by hand: the set has no weapon or armour table, no
   ranges (range comes from the player's words, P5-27), no weight, no rate of fire and no properties. AC is not
   computed from armour, shield and Dex in non-SRD sets (the SRD path does compute it). Strength damage is typed into
   the formula (P5-36).
5. **Is weight or encumbrance tracked, and does it change movement?** No. Sheets have no items or weights
   (regole/schede.ts:21-57), and the Engine tracks neither movement nor distance (docs/combattimento.md §6). Every
   row of P4-03 to P4-08 is absent.
6. **How are roll scores computed, and do they match the manual?**
   - **Ability modifier**: the set's table (regole/schede.ts:81-92). It is exact (3: −3; 4–5: −2; 6–8: −1;
     9–12: 0; 13–15: +1; 16–17: +2; 18: +3).
   - **Attack**: d20 + the table bonus of Str (melee) or Dex (missile) + the sheet's attack bonus, against the
     target's AC (prove/risolutore-regole.ts:270-273, :368-371). This is right, but the level part is hand-written
     and never progresses (P5-19).
   - **Saves**: d20 + (17 − table number) against 17. This is the same as the manual's d20 ≥ number. Naturals 20
     and 1 count on attacks and saves.
   - **Situational bonuses**: the set's `modificatori` (+2 from behind, +2 charge with double damage, +1 short
     range, −2 long range) apply by the words of a PC's action. Anything else gets the arbiter's flat ±2.
   - **Where the numbers differ from the manual**:
     - blindness ±2 instead of −4/−4 (P5-54a);
     - cover a flat −2 instead of −1 to −8 (P5-29);
     - held: +2 for everyone instead of −4 AC against others (P5-43a);
     - range by words, not by distance (P5-27);
     - death save a fixed 10 instead of the PC's own save (P8-14);
     - morale's first check and its group roll (P5-45);
     - reaction a d20 yes/no instead of 2d6 in four bands (P5-05);
     - surprise per side, not per creature (P5-04);
     - the ability-roll target stuck at 17 above level 1 (P8-18);
     - poison, charm and illusion saves without Con, Wis or Int (P5-61b);
     - non-thieves listening on d20 ≥ 17 instead of 1 in 6 (P4-12);
     - one NPC attack per round (P6-07);
     - fists 1d4 lethal instead of 1d3 or 1d4 subduing (P5-42).

## Part 6: Monsters (pp. 62–162)

**How many.** The manual has about 200 monster entries: 201 «Armor Class:» lines in the plain text of pp. 63–162,
one per stat block. Many blocks have two or three columns (giant, huge and large ants; the seven age categories in
each dragon's table), so the variants number about 270–320: 270 class words on the «Save As:» lines and about 320
values on the «XP:» lines, counted in layout mode. The count is approximate. The framework has no bestiary: the
Preparer writes every NPC and minion sheet by hand from the manual. torre-mozza has two monster sheets (a hobgoblin,
png_grosso; a goblin, png_zirra) and no minion types.
