---
titolo: "Ability checks"
parole: [check, roll, skill, proficiency, modifier, ability, succeed, fail]
---
A check is made when a character attempts something with a real chance of failing and the outcome matters. Roll a d20 and add the ability modifier; if the character is proficient in the skill used, also add the Proficiency Bonus (+2 at levels 1–4). If the total reaches the DC the check succeeds. A natural 20 does not make a check succeed on its own.
