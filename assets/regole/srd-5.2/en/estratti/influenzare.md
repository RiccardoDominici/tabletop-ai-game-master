---
titolo: "Influencing an NPC"
parole: [convince, persuade, persuasion, deceive, lie, lying, deception, intimidate, intimidation, threaten, influence, social]
---
Someone who already wants to do what you ask needs no convincing; someone who would never do it is not convinced by the die. If the NPC hesitates, roll Persuasion, Deception, Intimidation or Performance against DC 15, or the NPC's Intelligence if higher. A friendly NPC gives advantage, a hostile one disadvantage.
