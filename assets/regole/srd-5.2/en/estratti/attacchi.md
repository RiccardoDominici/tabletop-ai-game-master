To hit, roll a d20 plus the attack bonus (the weapon's ability plus proficiency) against the target's Armor Class. On a natural 20 the attack always hits and is a Critical Hit: the damage dice are rolled twice. On a natural 1 it always misses. A ranged weapon has disadvantage if an enemy is right next to you.
