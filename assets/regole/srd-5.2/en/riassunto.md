# How to play (SRD 5.2)

When the outcome of an action is uncertain and failure matters, you roll a **d20**: the result plus the modifier must reach the **Difficulty Class (DC)**. The Engine rolls the dice, and the card shows the roll on the projector and on the phone.

## The roll
- **Ability check**: d20 + the ability modifier (Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma) + the Proficiency Bonus if the character is proficient in the skill (Persuasion, Athletics, Stealth…).
- **DC**: very easy 5, easy 10, medium 15, hard 20, very hard 25, nearly impossible 30.
- **Advantage**: roll two d20s and keep the higher; **disadvantage**: the lower. Together they cancel out.
- A natural 20 and a natural 1 count only for attacks.

## Fighting
- **Attack**: d20 + the weapon's bonus against the target's **Armor Class (AC)**. A natural 20 is a Critical Hit (damage dice doubled), a natural 1 always misses.
- Damage takes away **Hit Points**; at 0 an NPC is out of the fight, a PC falls unconscious.

## Talking to NPCs
- Convincing, deceiving or intimidating an NPC who hesitates: a check against DC 15 or its Intelligence, if higher. A friendly NPC gives advantage, a hostile one disadvantage. Someone who already wants to do it needs no convincing; someone who would never do it is not convinced by the die.

## Helping
- Helping a companion with what they are doing gives them advantage.
